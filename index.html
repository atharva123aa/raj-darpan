The lawn at Raj Darpan

A wide open lawn at the back of the hall, lit up in the evening, with room for a stage, a dance floor and as many tables as the day needs.

The lawn

We put up drapes in whatever colours your family wants, string lights over the grass, and set the stage at one end. The DJ truss, speakers and checkered floor in this photo went up for one night's party.

Sunset events work especially well here, when the lights come on and the grass is still warm.

The covered hall

If the weather turns, the party moves inside. The hall has an arched stage wall, drapes, and mood lighting, and opens straight onto the lawn.
